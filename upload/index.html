Anti-AI Writing Research

How to spot machine writing.

Researchers have spent years studying what makes AI text feel off. The answer is rarely one word. It is usually a pattern: too many smooth transitions, sentences that are all the same length, examples that could apply to anything, and a tone that never takes a side.

Below is a field guide. Every pattern JK-TECH-CODE looks for.

Category 3

Transitions that give it away

  • Furthermore
  • Moreover
  • Additionally
  • Nevertheless
  • Consequently
  • Thus
  • In contrast
  • As a result

Humans say: also, plus, but, still, yet, so, anyway.

Category 4

Email habits AI leans on

Openers: "I hope this finds you well." "I trust you are doing well."

Closers: "Please dont hesitate to reach out." "Thank you for your time and consideration."

Real emails start with "Hi name." Real emails end with "Thanks."

Category 7

Weak introductions and endings

AI intros often start with: "In todays fast-paced world..." or "AI has transformed..."

AI conclusions just repeat what was already said. Often starting with "In conclusion" or "To sum up."

A good ending adds something new. Or ends with a question. Or stops before overstaying.

Category 8

Structure habits

AI writes in neat blocks. Introduction. Point one and example. Point two and example. Point three and example. Conclusion.

Every paragraph is roughly the same length. Every sentence feels measured.

Real writing is uneven. Some paragraphs are short. Some ramble. Good writing does not need to be symmetrical.

Category 9

Conversational rewrites

It is important to note that effective communication plays a pivotal role in fostering successful relationships.

AI version

human version

AI version

human version
Things change fast. People who adapt do better.
AI version
The key takeaway is that consistency drives long-term success.
human version
Small habits, repeated over time, usually beat big one-off efforts.
Category 10

What AI rarely does

Humans contradict themselves. They change tone mid-paragraph. They use strange metaphors. They tell messy stories. They add irrelevant details. They leave thoughts unfinished. They express uncertainty without fixing it.

AI smooths all of that out. That smoothness is the biggest tell.

FAQ

Common questions.

Does JK-TECH-CODE store my writing? +

No. Your text is analyzed in memory and never written to disk. Once you close the browser tab, it is gone.

Can JK-TECH-CODE detect ChatGPT, Gemini, and Claude? +

JK-TECH-CODE does not try to identify which model wrote something. It looks for patterns common across all AI-generated text. That approach works regardless of the model.

Can I just use JK-TECH-CODE to rewrite my AI drafts? +

Yes. That is exactly what it is for. Paste AI-generated text. JK-TECH-CODE flags what sounds robotic. You rewrite those parts. The result sounds like you again.

Is this a plagiarism checker? +

No. JK-TECH-CODE does not check for copied content. It checks for writing patterns that feel machine-generated. Two different things.

Does it work for non-English text? +

Currently JK-TECH-CODE is built for English. Support for other languages is under development.
